## 1. Direction: open air

The visitor is standing outside, in front of concrete that plants are taking back. The scene is always there; the content is built into it.

- **The scene is the page.** `RouteBackground` stays fixed and **visible for the whole scroll**, on every route. Use the `bg-*-sharp.webp` files (they are the backgrounds the owner chose; the current blurred bakes are too veiled to recognise). Apply only a light blur (about 4 px at 1440) and no mint veil above 15 %. Slight parallax on scroll (max 40 px of travel). Case studies use the Projects scene.
- **No flat mint page.** `--mist` is only fog and sky inside the scene. `body` shows the scene.
- **Content sits on concrete walls.** Each content section is a large concrete plane (texture from V3 block A) spanning the content width: a wall, a terrace, a slab. Not small boxes behind paragraphs: one structural surface per section, with generous padding. Between walls there are gaps where the scene shows through. The hero has no wall.
- **Plants take the walls.** Every wall has growth on at least one edge: moss along the top joint, grass at the base, a vine crossing a corner. Build these as reusable overlay pieces (SVG or baked sprites from the fur engine), placed with a seeded random so no two walls match. Decorative only, `aria-hidden`, never over text.
- **It moves a little.** A slow fog layer drifting across the scene, and a few petals falling now and then (lightweight, one shared canvas or CSS, paused when the tab is hidden). Off with `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## B. Layout: truly responsive

1. Delete `--content-max: 1104px` and every centred fixed-width column.
2. The content area is the viewport minus the nav. Side padding is fluid: `clamp(16px, 4vw, 96px)`. Sections use the full width: grids are `auto-fit` with `minmax`, so a 2560 px screen gets more columns and larger pieces, not more empty margin.
3. Type scales with the viewport up to 2560 px (`clamp`). Running text keeps 45–75 characters per line by living in columns inside a wider composition, never by narrowing the whole page.
4. The hero bust scales with viewport height and stays centred at every width.
5. Test and screenshot at: 390×844, 768×1024, 1024×768, 1366×768, 1440×900, 1920×1080, 2560×1440, 3440×1440. At each one: no horizontal scroll, no overlap, no section narrower than 70 % of the content area on screens 1440 and wider.

## D. Buttons: two types

1. **Velour Bolster (type 1).** Used for "See projects", "All projects" and the nav. **On hover the label glows**: soft light bloom around the letters (`--mist` glow, eased in 200 ms). Drop the rule "one primary per screen".
2. **Mohair Capsule (type 2).** Used for every other button. **Lighter green fur** (`--moss-200`, roots `--moss-700`), dark label. **The label must react to the cursor the way the social icons do**: draw the label into the fur as its artwork, so the letters part and ripple under the pointer. Keep a real, visually hidden text label for assistive tech and keep the element an `<a>` or `<button>`.
3. No third style anywhere.

## E. Botanical Text: local, no fade

Applies to every H1 and H2, and to the hero role line.

- **The real text never disappears, fades or is replaced.**
- Leaves and flowers **grow only on the part of the text under the cursor**: a radius of about one capital height around the pointer. They follow the pointer along the word, stay for about 1.2 s after it leaves that spot, then wilt. Moving across the title leaves a trail of growth.
- The canvas sits exactly over the text: same position, same metrics, at every size. Verify by rendering all particles and checking they fall inside the glyph bounds (the Home title currently grows in the wrong place: fix).
- **Legibility:** within the active radius, growth covers at most 55 % of each glyph, so the word is always readable through it.
- **Flowers in four colours**: `--flower-pink`, `--flower-yellow`, `--flower-violet`, `--flower-white`. Flowers are at most 20 % of the particles; the rest is foliage in the moss greens.
- Phone and touch: plain text.
- **Fallback, only if the local effect cannot be made to look right:** keep the original OriginKit behaviour, but fix density so every title is readable, and use the four flower colours. Say so in the report.
